How the Book Finder works

  1. Every book on our shelf has a short description, genres, and mood words.
  2. A small AI language model (MiniLM, run with Transformers.js) turns each description into a list of 384 numbers that captures its meaning.
  3. When you type what you want, the model turns your words into numbers the same way.
  4. The app compares your request with every book and shows the ones with the closest meaning, even if they don't share the same words.

The model runs entirely in your browser. What you type is never sent to a server. The first visit downloads the model (about 23 MB); after that it loads from your phone's cache.
